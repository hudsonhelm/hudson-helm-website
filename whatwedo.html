Managed IT Services

Your technology should support the work, not become another job to manage. Hudson Helm provides ongoing oversight, maintenance, and coordination so the systems your team depends on stay reliable.

  • Workstation, laptop, and basic server management
  • Patch management, standardization, and routine maintenance
  • Documentation and coordination with internet, software, and equipment vendors
  • Right-sized support without the overhead of a full internal IT department

Responsive Support

When an employee cannot work, a ticket number is not enough. We troubleshoot clearly, follow issues through, and help people get back to business without unnecessary jargon or runaround.

  • Remote and on-site troubleshooting when appropriate
  • Help with devices, printers, applications, logins, and everyday issues
  • Escalation and follow-through for problems that need deeper investigation
  • User onboarding, offboarding, and access changes

Cloud & Email

Email, calendars, shared files, and cloud accounts sit at the center of daily work. We handle the administration behind those tools so access stays organized, secure, and aligned with how your team operates.

  • Microsoft 365 setup, licensing, migrations, and cleanup
  • Mailbox, calendar, Teams, OneDrive, and SharePoint support
  • Account permissions, identity settings, and multi-factor authentication
  • Cloud-service coordination and practical configuration guidance

Cybersecurity

Small-business security works best as a maintained set of sensible layers. We focus on reducing common risks with controls that protect the business while letting employees remain productive.

  • Endpoint protection, patching, and baseline hardening
  • Multi-factor authentication and access-control guidance
  • Email and account security improvements
  • Clear, practical steps to reduce user and configuration risk

Backup & Recovery

A backup matters only if it is current, monitored, and recoverable. We help protect important systems and data, then plan how the business can restore what it needs after a failure or mistake.

  • Backup planning for devices, servers, and cloud data
  • Monitoring and review of backup status
  • Recovery-readiness checks and practical restore planning
  • Clear priorities for protecting business-critical information

Network & Wi-Fi

Reliable connectivity is the foundation beneath nearly every business system. We set up, troubleshoot, and improve the network so employees and devices can connect securely and consistently.

  • Firewall, switch, and wireless access-point setup
  • Wi-Fi coverage, performance, and connectivity troubleshooting
  • Network refreshes and practical equipment recommendations
  • Coordination with internet providers and cabling vendors

Projects & Consulting

Some technology work needs a defined plan rather than an open-ended support ticket. Hudson Helm keeps projects organized, communicates what is changing, and handles the details from preparation through completion.

  • Email, file, platform, and device migrations
  • Office moves, network refreshes, and infrastructure upgrades
  • Technology assessments and environment cleanup
  • Planning, vendor coordination, and implementation support

Strategic IT Guidance

Good technology decisions start with understanding the business, its risks, and where it is heading. We help owners turn competing needs into a practical plan and make informed choices about providers, systems, and investments.

  • Prioritized technology roadmaps and budgeting guidance
  • Vendor and solution evaluation without unnecessary complexity
  • Planning for growth, staffing changes, and new locations
  • Clear recommendations connected to business goals and risk
