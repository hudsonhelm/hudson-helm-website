Are we a good fit?

Hudson Helm is built for organizations that depend on technology every day but do not want a giant, impersonal IT provider. We do our best work with businesses that need responsive support, practical guidance, and reliable systems—without turning every small request into a project.

We may be a good fit if...

We tend to be a strong match for professional offices and established small businesses that want real support, not just ticket taking - leading to the productive long-term relationships.

You have 5-50 employees

Your team is large enough to depend on technology, but not large enough to need an internal IT department.

Your business depends on technology every day

Reliable computers, cloud services, connectivity, and business systems are essential to keeping work moving.

You value responsive support

Your staff benefits from accessible remote help and practical onsite support when the situation calls for it.

You take security seriously

You are willing to make sensible improvements that protect people, information, and business continuity.

You prefer practical advice

You prefer clear recommendations shaped by business needs and budget—not complexity for its own sake.

You want an ongoing relationship

You value consistency, ownership, and familiar people over anonymous ticket handling and one-off fixes.

We May Not Be The Right Fit If...

A clear fit works both ways. Another provider may serve you better if your priorities or preferred support model look like these.

  • Your only priority is finding the lowest-cost break/fix provider.

  • Basic maintenance and security are not investments your business is prepared to make.

  • You require the scale and structure of a large national or enterprise help desk.

  • You want IT to remain entirely reactive rather than becoming more organized and proactive.

Not sure yet? A straightforward conversation is a good place to start.

Tell us how your business works, where technology is getting in the way, and what better support would look like.
